Our Story

Your Home Away From Home

Juniper Cove Bar & Grill is Culver's favourite family-oriented restaurant and sports bar. We're the kind of place where you walk in as a stranger and leave as a regular — warm, welcoming, and always ready to serve up something delicious.

From our perfectly seared top sirloin and hand-battered onion rings to our legendary Eggs Benedict on weekend mornings — every dish is made with care and served with a smile.
